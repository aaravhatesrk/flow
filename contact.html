Questions

The questions we get asked most

Will it make things up about my business?
No. The assistant can only answer from the knowledge base we build with you. If a visitor asks something it was not given, it says so and offers to put them through to you on WhatsApp instead of guessing.
What happens to the details people leave?
They go to your WhatsApp and email immediately, and into a Google Sheet that you own. We ask for consent before saving anything, and we do not sell or reuse your leads.
Do I need a developer to install it?
No. If you already have a website, it is one line of code that we can send to whoever manages it. If you do not have one, we build the site too.
What does it cost to keep running?
The website is a one-time build fee — see the exact tiers on our pricing page. The assistant is a flat ₹2,499/month plan with unlimited conversations under fair use — no tiers. There is no charge per conversation and no setup fee on the assistant.
Can I cancel?
Yes — plans are month to month. You keep the website and everything we wrote for you. Annual billing gets you two months free if you prefer to pay in one go.
Is it only for coaching institutes?
That is where we started, and where our live reference client is, but the same setup works for clinics, salons, studios, tuition centres and local service businesses.
